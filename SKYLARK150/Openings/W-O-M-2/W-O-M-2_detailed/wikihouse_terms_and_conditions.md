## CNC Information

All geometry has tolerance offsets pre-applied for manufacture. "Dog-bone" pockets have been added on internal corners to mitigate any filleting caused by the router bit. Follow the layer information below for guidance on recommended settings.

---

## Layer Information

| Layer | Description |
|---|---|
| `DONOTCUT` | Information for instruction purposes only |
| `0_SHEET_SPRUCEPLY_2440X1220X18` | Plywood material boundary for reference |
| `1_ANYTOOL_1MMDEEP_LABELS` | Text labels — convert to curves and engrave at 1 mm depth |
| `2_ANYTOOL_1MMDEEP_SCREWMARKS` | Marker "dinks" for nails/screws during assembly — pre-mark using 2–3 mm bit |
| `3_ANYTOOL_CUTTHROUGH_INSIDE` | Internal slots — cut through inside line |
| `4_ANYTOOL_CUTTHROUGH_OUTSIDE` | External profile of parts — cut through outside line |
| `5_ANYTOOL_HALF_MILL_9MM_INSIDE` | Mill out area to 9 mm depth |
